Parameters and criteria: tasks/s6_execution.md. Each seed runs four arms on one
phase-noise stream: a gradient step, a symmetric random step at the Frobenius norm
of that arm's own gradient, that arm's own gradient step under a random node
relabelling, and a frozen kernel. Every direction is read off the arm's own phases
and couplings, so the controls follow the gradient arm's rule and none receives its
step; the sizes that rule produces are comparable rather than equal once the
kernels diverge. Squared drift is a dissipation function, not entropy production.
The cluster Gram template is diagnostic only.

Diagnostics are sampled at the update cadence, at the instant each update has
just acted, so every objective figure below is a mean of that post-update readout
and not a time mean over the intervals between updates. The descent fraction is
the share of the frozen arm's headroom above the drive floor N*mean(omega)^2/D
that an arm removes on that readout. The within/between ratio and the
template correlation are invariant under rescaling the kernel; Frobenius distance
to a template is not, and a fixed resource total does not hold the kernel norm
fixed. The permutation percentile is the share of node relabellings whose
template the final kernel sits closer to than it does to the true one. Kernel norm
growth is the final over the initial Frobenius norm, and measures how far
clip-and-rescale has concentrated the fixed resource onto fewer edges.

Two partitions are scored on the same kernels. Within/between is the frequency
partition, which is the structure the environment carries. Interleaved is a
partition of the same group sizes balanced against it, carrying no frequency
information. The blind percentile reads the frequency partition's ratio against
the whole family of such partitions: it is the share of frequency-blind
relabellings whose ratio is lower still, so a value near zero says the loss of
within-cluster coupling belongs to the frequency partition rather than to block
structure at large. Neither partition enters the update.

The production rate is the one at which the objective descends furthest while the
run still meets the declared plateau criterion; larger rates lose descent without
failing that criterion, so the sweep and not the criterion is what selects a rate.

All gradient runs maintain sampled r > 0.5 after t=10 and pass the declared 5%
plateau criterion. The post-update objective sits well below the frozen arm's;
neither control arm does, so that reduction and the loss of alignment are specific
to the gradient rather than to motion of the kernel at fixed total resource.
Within-cluster coupling falls relative to between-cluster coupling, and the final
kernel is further from the true template than from nearly every node relabelling:
this run does not exhibit structural resonance.

Only the gradient arm's frequency partition is unusual against frequency-blind
partitions of the same group sizes. Its interleaved ratio and those of the
control arms sit inside the blind family, so the descent moves against the
structure the environment carries rather than dissolving block structure of any
kind: it is opposed to that structure, not indifferent to structure.

The two controls separate two things that the random arm alone confounds. Fresh
isotropic steps cancel, so that arm ends far less deformed than the gradient arm
and does not enter the sparsification channel that clip-and-rescale opens. The
permuted arm takes its own gradient step under a random node relabelling: a
relabelling is an isometry that leaves the multiset of step entries alone, so the
arm deforms the kernel over a range that brackets the gradient arm's and differs
from it in which edges the step reaches. Its kernel norm growth, within/between
ratio and permutation percentile are the columns to read against the gradient
arm's, and the overlap of the ranges is what the reading rests on.

Minimizing squared drift under a conserved mean drift equalizes drift across
nodes, which rewards coupling between clusters of unlike frequency. The template
rewards coupling within clusters of like frequency. That the objective's optimum
and the template disagree is a property of this pairing, not a general result
about dissipation.

These finite seeded trajectories establish neither monotonic descent nor
stationarity of the adaptive process, phase locking in the presence of noise,
a thermodynamic entropy-production rate, or any E45/Lean obligation.
No timestep convergence is inferred, and the learning rate is selected by the
sweep above rather than by a convergence argument.
