# Archive — superseded working documents

**Nothing in this directory is current. Where any file here conflicts with
`main.tex` or `supplementary.tex`, the manuscript governs.**

These are earlier planning documents, ledgers and drafts, kept because the
active ledger (`tasks/todo.md`) and `CHANGELOG.md` cite them by path as the
record of how decisions were reached. They are history, not claims.

## Read this before quoting anything here

Several of these documents were written for an earlier and much stronger
version of the project — provisionally titled *The Inevitability of Mind* —
whose central claims the current article **explicitly does not make**. The
retractions are not stylistic; they are the substance of what changed:

| An archived document says | The current article says |
| :--- | :--- |
| The corollary "disqualifies rigid von Neumann architectures (like GPUs)" | "No conclusion about artificial consciousness follows." The two hardware results are a statement about constrained optimization and a property of a measure, and one of them cuts against this framework's own coarse-graining step. |
| The cerebellum is "massive empirical proof" that "crystalline architectures cannot achieve the structural resonance required for consciousness" | No such argument appears. Nothing in the sheaf or fixed-point conditions is specific to cortex or to electromagnetic coupling. |
| Consciousness "is impossible on rigid, abstract-symbolic substrates" | The substrate claim is empirical and is carried entirely by two named hypotheses, neither of which is established here. |
| The framework is an "axiomatic derivation from first principles" | The Lean development declares no axioms of its own, and the composition is one *conditional* theorem consuming eight named hypotheses. Nothing derives consciousness from first principles. |

`publication_strategy.md` names journals that are no longer the target and
reasons about their referees; it reflects an early framing of the work and not
a current submission plan.

`ecc_impact_analysis.md` refers to Energetically Coherent Computation (ECC), a
separate project by the same author, published at
<https://github.com/bobaseb/energetically-coherent-computation> and licensed
CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. It is cited there by a relative checkout path, which will not
resolve for anyone reading this repository; use the link. No ECC text or code
is vendored into this repository.

The archived `todo_*.md` ledgers are the most reliable files here: they are
dated pass records, each superseded by the next, and the active ledger names
them explicitly. Their closed items are accurate as of their dates.

## Why keep any of it

The article's method is to name what it assumes and what it cannot show. A
record of the claims that were tried and then withdrawn is part of that, and
withdrawing them quietly would be the opposite. The point of this file is that
a reader can tell which is which without having to reconstruct the order.
