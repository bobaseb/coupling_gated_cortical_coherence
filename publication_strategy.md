# Publication Strategy

## Target Journal
**Primary Target:** *Entropy* (MDPI)

*   **Why:** *Entropy* is highly receptive to theoretical, mathematically dense position papers. It is a central hub for literature bridging information theory, thermodynamics, statistical mechanics, and cognitive science. They frequently publish "Hypothesis" or "Perspective" papers and are open to novel formalisms. Reviewers here are likely to view the rigorous formalization (Lean derivations) of thermodynamic limits on computation as a major methodological strength rather than an alien concept.

## Alternative Options
If *Entropy* does not pan out, the following venues are strong backups for this proof-of-principle position paper:

1. **Frontiers in Computational Neuroscience**
   *   **Track:** "Hypothesis and Theory"
   *   **Why:** They explicitly seek position papers. Given the computational focus of the journal, the formal verification (Lean) of physical impossibilities in rigid AI substrates would be highly relevant and well-received.

2. **Journal of Artificial Intelligence and Consciousness (JAIC)**
   *   **Why:** Ideal if the paper is framed primarily as an argument about the physical prerequisites for true artificial sentience (mortal computation) and a critique of current von Neumann/LLM architectures.

## Lean Derivations Strategy
To ensure the paper is readable for physicists and biologists while maintaining mathematical rigor:
*   **Main Text:** Focus on the physics, prose, and the overarching position. Present theorems (e.g., Kuramoto phase-locking, Landauer erasure constraints) in standard mathematical notation.
*   **Proof of Principle:** Explicitly state in the methodology that the core thermodynamic feedback loops and bounds have been formally verified using the Lean interactive theorem prover to ensure strict physical rigor.
*   **Code / Appendices:** Include short, elegant snippets of Lean definitions directly in the text to demonstrate the formalism, but relegate the heavy derivation code to an Appendix and this GitHub repository.
