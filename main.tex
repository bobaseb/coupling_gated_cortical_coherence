\begin{abstract}
\singlespacing
How could activity distributed across cortex support one experienced situation? We separate three requirements: \emph{coherence} (collective phase order), \emph{compatibility} (local descriptions agree where their domains overlap) and \emph{self-representation} (the state carries an accurate reconstruction of itself). Coherence supplies neither of the others, and five results establish that with no cortical hypothesis: synchrony constrains content only through encoders shared on overlaps, and uniformly over pairs only inside a patch too small to span the territories at issue; the resultant does not determine the state; a unique fixed point is not a self-model; where the encoding is a region's restriction, the region must resolve the relevant family before any readout is chosen; and agreement about a change takes communication. That grain is the uniform bound's, not phase order's: three results replace it by a count of the pairs that can disagree, a chain across a cover's shared sites, or a spectral gap at a frequency-locked configuration, each against a declared input coherence does not supply. Four are checkable for a machine candidate, fixing what a claim of machine unity commits its maker to measuring. Under stated hypotheses, compatible local states glue to a unique global state that an accurate contracting reconstruction fixes uniquely; installed energy bounds a declared actuator's mean coupling, so a coherent branch requires it to exceed $2D/\kappa$, for a $\kappa$ derived here for neither cortex nor a processor. A Lean~4 development carries these results and their composition, with the eight connecting hypotheses as explicit arguments, no incomplete proofs and an audited axiom footprint. One prediction needs no absolute calibration: onset delay scales as the square root of the rate at which coupling crosses threshold, which a within-subject emergence-from-anaesthesia design can test. We propose the glued state and its internal reconstruction as correlates of unity and a minimal self, and an extracellular-field hypothesis linking awakening to rising coupling; testing it requires calibrated geometry, resolved phase dynamics and validated decoders.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

How could activity distributed across cortex support one experience? A field account proposes that endogenous extracellular electromagnetic fields partly coordinate spatially separated neural processes \cite{mcfadden2002,mcfadden2020,pockett2012}. Ephaptic effects provide a possible interaction mechanism \cite{frohlich2010,anastassiou2015}. Successful coordination would still leave a representational question: what makes the coordinated activity a description of one situation, with a perspective on that situation?

We distinguish three requirements. \emph{Coherence} concerns collective phase order. \emph{Compatibility} concerns whether local descriptions agree where their domains overlap. \emph{Self-representation} concerns whether the resulting state carries an accurate internal reconstruction of itself. We propose the latter two relations as formal correlates of conscious unity and a minimal self; their interpretation requires evidence beyond the phase model.

Unity has been approached differently elsewhere: integrated information theory treats integration and exclusion as constitutive \cite{tononi2016}, global-workspace accounts tie availability to broadcast \cite{baars1997,dehaene2014}, and predictive-processing accounts derive both from a hierarchical generative model \cite{friston2010}. Here the three requirements are kept apart rather than derived from one another, so that each carries its own assumptions and admits its own measurement; Section~\ref{sec:scope} returns to the comparison.

Consider reaching for a cup. A visual description of its location and a description of the hand's relation to it can agree about where the cup is while contributing different contents. Synchronizing the two processes would leave disagreement about that shared quantity unresolved. An internal representation must additionally locate the perceiver within the represented situation. This example motivates the requirements; the mathematical constructions use spatial measures and fields in real normed vector spaces, whose identification with perceptual content is a further modelling task.

The mathematical contribution is to specify connections between these requirements and the assumptions each connection needs. A declared actuator's stored energy bounds its mean coupling. Phase encoders shared on overlaps convert phase order into a bound on content disagreement. Compatible spatial measures have a unique global extension; approximate agreement controls a specified global selection. A contracting reconstruction makes that extension its unique fixed point only if it accurately reconstructs the extension. Accuracy across a family of states imposes a further condition on the information retained by the encoding.

The empirical contribution is to identify what observations can decide. Stationary phase distributions can be shared by stronger coupling, weaker diffusion and a matched common drive. Finite ramps change the observable onset. A winding field is stationary at the same coupling as a uniform one and has the opposite resultant, so that observable does not determine the state. Coherent adaptation can lose alignment with a designated environmental template, and a compatibility estimator needs diagnostics that distinguish content from decoder artifacts. These findings determine the measurements needed to test the proposed connections.

Three of the ingredients are standard: the synchronization threshold $K_c=2D$ for the noisy mean-field model \cite{sakaguchi1988}, the unique gluing of compatible local sections, and the Banach fixed-point theorem \cite{banach1922}. Four things here are not. The installed-energy condition makes coupling a purchased quantity: a declared actuator's stored energy bounds the mean coupling it supplies, so coherence on the stationary branch carries a resource requirement in units a measurement can reach, and for cortex that requirement reduces to a figure the mode prices must deliver. The coherence-to-content bound converts phase order into a statement about descriptions rather than about timing, and its patch-local form is what keeps it from being vacuous in a large population. A stability criterion on the reweighted coupling kernel, together with an existence condition on an amplitude layer, settles when a low resultant reports weak coupling and when it reports a winding field. And the composition is machine-checked together with a witness discharging all eight hypotheses at once, which is what makes their conjunction non-empty rather than a list no object satisfies.

Granting those premises, collected in Table~\ref{tab:summary}, the account runs as follows. Extracellular contraction on awakening raises endogenous field amplitude and with it the effective coupling between cortical oscillators. Once that coupling passes $2D$, a coherent phase branch exists, and from an initial spread within a quarter turn the population settles onto it; other initial data leave windings stationary at the same coupling. Encoders shared where cortical territories overlap turn that phase order into agreement about the quantities the territories describe in common, so the local descriptions glue: there is one state of which each is a restriction. Part of that state encodes the state itself, and a readout of that part reconstructs it accurately enough, and sensitively enough across the states that matter, to make it the unique state the system models itself as being. On this account the glued state is what makes distributed activity one situation, and its accurate self-reconstruction is what gives that situation a perspective. Every step of it is conditional.

Section~\ref{sec:unconditional} collects the results that hold whatever physical field is proposed and whatever substrate carries it; a reader who rejects the cortical hypothesis of Section~\ref{sec:prediction} still has to accommodate them. Sections~\ref{sec:field}--\ref{sec:self} then develop the three requirements together (Fig.~\ref{fig:chain}), and Section~\ref{sec:gpu} reads the unconditional results against a machine candidate. Section~\ref{sec:chain} composes them, stating the thermodynamic constraints and all eight connecting hypotheses per branch; its supporting models of feedback, learning and resource supply have distinct state spaces and assumptions. Section~\ref{sec:observations} then examines what the observables can establish, before Section~\ref{sec:prediction} develops the cortical hypothesis and an awakening experiment. The hypothesis is that extracellular contraction increases field-mediated coupling; its direction, rate and relation to recovery require independent calibration. Supplemental Material organizes the constructions and controls behind the claims they support. Table~\ref{tab:notation}, at the end, lists the symbols and the section that defines each.

\section{What holds without the cortical hypothesis}
\label{sec:unconditional}

Most of what follows is conditional. Table~\ref{tab:summary} names eight hypotheses that the composition consumes rather than derives, and the cortical identification of Section~\ref{sec:prediction} is a further commitment on top of them. Five results are not conditional in that way. They constrain any account that reads unity off phase coherence, whatever physical field is proposed to carry the coupling and whatever substrate carries the field. Each is stated below where it is used; collected here, they are the article's fixed points.

\paragraph{Phase order constrains content only inside a patch too small to carry unity.} Assign every patch the same phase and let two local mass profiles disagree at a shared site. The profiles are chosen independently of phase, so synchrony places no constraint on their disagreement, and they have no common extension: perfect locking is consistent with local descriptions that do not glue (Section~\ref{sec:unity}). What the positive result of Section~\ref{sec:content-connection} costs is exactly this gap. A bound on content disagreement needs encoders already related on overlaps, and that relation is a supplied fact about the descriptions rather than a consequence of the dynamics. Supplying it relocates the limit rather than lifting it. Eq.~\eqref{eq:main-content-coherence} bounds every pair at once and a chord never exceeds $2$, so at the patch-local order of the sheet in Section~\ref{sec:wave} that uniform reading carries information only at \wavePatchInformativeSites{} sites or fewer. The grain is the uniform reading's and not phase order's, and Section~\ref{sec:content-connection} states three results without it: a scale-free count of the pairs that can disagree, which names none of them; a bound chained through the sites a cover's patches share, which travels a distance linear in the hops taken; and, at a frequency-locked configuration, a bound read off the coupling graph's algebraic connectivity, which carries no population count at all. What none of the three changes is the constraint stated here: a count locates no site, and the other two consume a declared cover or declared hardware data, so phase order by itself still says nothing about territories too far apart to share a patch, which is where the compatibility claim is made.

\paragraph{The order parameter does not determine the state.} A field winding once across a periodic sheet has resultant $r=0$ --- the order parameter of Eq.~\eqref{eq:kuramoto} below --- while every patch of it stays locked, and this is not a contrived initial condition. On a periodic array of any dimension, a winding is stationary for \emph{any} isotropic symmetric kernel at identical frequencies, because the coupling term is odd in the separation against a kernel even in it (\texttt{char\_\allowbreak is\_\allowbreak kuramoto\_\allowbreak trajectory}). Section~\ref{sec:wave} exhibits a sheet on which uniform and winding initial conditions reach $\waveBandControlOrder$ and $\waveBandOrder$ at one coupling, diffusion and kernel. A low resultant therefore reports either weak coupling or a winding field, and no measurement in $r$ alone separates them. The state also carries an integer the resultant does not: read around a ring, the same winding has winding number $q$, and a nonzero $q$ obstructs every global real-valued phase lift (\texttt{winding\_\allowbreak degree\_\allowbreak obstructs}). The resultant is an average over sites and moves when they are reweighted or the patches redrawn; the degree telescopes and does not.

\paragraph{A unique fixed point is not a self-model.} A constant readout contracts, fixes whatever state it returns, and reconstructs nothing. Uniqueness is consequently the wrong criterion, and Section~\ref{sec:reconstruction-family} gives the right shape of one: reconstructing $M$ states pairwise more than $2\varepsilon$ apart within error $\varepsilon$ requires $M$ distinguishable codes, so an alphabet of $2^b$ codes caps the family at $M\leq2^b$. This is a counting argument and holds for any encoder and readout whatever.

\paragraph{Where the encoding is a restriction, the region must resolve the family.} Read the code as the state's own restriction to a region and the criterion moves off the readout entirely. A boundary whose region reads two relevant states identically cannot reconstruct them more than $2\varepsilon$ apart (\texttt{not\_\allowbreak isRestrictionResonance\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak reconstructs}), and a resonant boundary that does reconstruct a separated family restricts injectively on it (\texttt{restrictToAvatar\_\allowbreak injOn\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak isRestrictionResonance}). Where the region sits is fixed before an encoding is chosen, so no readout supplies resolution the region lacks.

\paragraph{Agreement after a change takes communication.} In a synchronous network whose updates read only the site's own state and its declared neighbours' messages, two configurations agreeing on a site's causal past through $T$ rounds leave that site identical at round $T$. Two interventions outside that past that require reports more than $2\varepsilon$ apart therefore cannot both be reported from it within $\varepsilon$ by the deadline (Section~\ref{sec:self}). Snapshot compatibility and timely compatibility are therefore different requirements, and cutting the path removes the second while leaving the first intact.

None of these depends on the coupling being electromagnetic, on the substrate being cortex, or on the eight hypotheses of Table~\ref{tab:summary} holding. Section~\ref{sec:gpu} applies four of them --- all but the one about the resultant, which is a statement about a spatial observable --- to a candidate that is neither biological nor field-coupled.

\section{Coherence: physical coupling and its resource condition}
\label{sec:field}

\subsection{Dynamics and the stationary threshold}

The finite stochastic model is
\begin{equation}
 d\theta_i=\left[\omega_i+\sum_j K_{ij}(t)\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i)\right]dt
 +\sqrt{2D}\,dW_i,
 \qquad
 r_N=\left|\frac1N\sum_i e^{i\theta_i}\right|.
 \label{eq:kuramoto}
\end{equation}
Here $\theta_i$ is the phase of oscillator $i$, $\omega_i$ its natural frequency, $D>0$ is phase diffusion, $W_i$ are independent Wiener processes, and $K_{ij}$ has inverse-time units, with angles dimensionless. Uniform mean-field coupling is $K_{ij}=K/N$; frequency heterogeneity and diffusion are distinct parameters. The stationary calculation below uses identical frequencies and this scalar coupling. Applying it to a spatial kernel requires an aggregation rule and a check of the resulting approximation.

For identical frequencies in their rotating frame, the positive normalized stationary densities of the one-body Fokker--Planck equation are exactly
\begin{equation}
 \rho(\theta)=\frac{e^{a\cos(\theta-\psi)}}{2\pi I_0(a)},
 \qquad a=\frac{Kr}{D},\qquad
 r=R(a):=\frac{I_1(a)}{I_0(a)},
 \label{eq:selfconsistency}
\end{equation}
where $I_n$ are modified Bessel functions and $\psi$ is the collective phase. The formal derivation uses the absence of stationary current for a periodic gradient drift to obtain this density, then verifies normalization and self-consistency. At $K\leq K_c=2D$, only $r=0$ is self-consistent \cite{sakaguchi1988}. Above threshold, exactly one positive solution coexists with zero and increases strictly with $K$. Its onset is
\begin{equation}
 \frac{r^2}{K-K_c}\longrightarrow\frac1D,
 \qquad r\sim\sqrt{\frac{K-K_c}{D}}.
 \label{eq:onset}
\end{equation}
Figure~\ref{fig:bifurcation} shows this stationary branch.

Linearization about the uniform density gives rate $K/2-D$ for the first harmonic and $-Dn^2$ for harmonics $n\geq2$. A growing mode therefore exists exactly above threshold. About the coherent density, the quadratic form is strictly dissipative transverse to collective rotation, whose rate is zero. These linear estimates concern the limiting equation. Relating them to finite stochastic trajectories requires a dynamical mean-field limit and branch selection; exchange symmetry alone does not establish the needed independence \cite{sznitman1991}.

\subsection{Installed energy bounds mean coupling}
\label{sec:installation}

A spatial model replaces the finite sum by integration against a kernel. One declared implementation carries response modes with continuous profiles $\phi_i$, installation prices $u_i$ and nonnegative occupancies $c_i(\Xi)$ in configuration $\Xi$:
\begin{equation}
 K_\Xi(x,y)=\sum_i c_i(\Xi)\phi_i(x)\phi_i(y),
 \qquad U(\Xi)=\sum_i u_i c_i(\Xi).
 \label{eq:microscopic-kernel}
\end{equation}
The same configuration sets the reciprocal kernel and stored energy. An executed transition changes the kernel through its changed occupancies and requires work $U(\Xi')-U(\Xi)+Q$, with $Q$ positive into the reservoir. The installed kernel is averaged over the resulting configuration law $p(\Xi)$. Cell-pair reconstruction on refining spatial partitions converges to its product-measure integral under the specified compactness and sampling assumptions; the refinement index is independent of the number of control updates.

Normalize the substrate measure $\mu$ to one. Write $m_i=\int\phi_i\,d\mu$ and declare $\kappa>0$ with $m_i^2\leq\kappa u_i$ for every mode. Factorizing each mode's integral gives
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 U_{\mathrm{inst}}&=\sum_\Xi p(\Xi)U(\Xi),\\
 K_{\mathrm{mf}}&=\iint K(x,y)\,d\mu(x)\,d\mu(y)
 =\sum_\Xi p(\Xi)\sum_i c_i(\Xi)m_i^2
 \leq\kappa U_{\mathrm{inst}}.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:installed-coupling-bound}
\end{equation}
If this mean coupling is the $K$ of the scalar model, $\kappa U_{\mathrm{inst}}\leq2D$ excludes a coherent stationary branch and any growing Fourier mode about the uniform density. Thus coherence on that branch requires $U_{\mathrm{inst}}>2D/\kappa$. The condition is necessary: energy above it need not produce sufficient coupling because the bound can be loose.

The quantity constrained is installed energy rather than dissipated heat, and the condition binds through $\kappa$ rather than through the energy, since any macroscopic substrate carries installed energy far above the thermal floor. Read forwards it therefore excludes no macroscopic candidate. Read backwards it is a number. Take the region the field arithmetic takes, a sphere of one LFP decay length holding $\energyNeurons$ neurons, at the metabolic rates Supplemental Material collects and over one phase-diffusion time $1/D=\energyResidence$~s at $D=\energyDiffusion$~rad$^2$/s: the installed energy is $\energyInstalled$~J, and the condition is met at every $\kappa$ above $\energyKappaRequired$~J$^{-1}$s$^{-1}$. That figure is what an identification of cortical mode prices has to deliver, and the first identification delivering less refutes the coupling-gated account for cortex at this region and interval. Another interval rescales the figure by its ratio to this one.

What the condition discriminates, it discriminates through that factor. The required $\kappa$ scales as $D^2$ and inversely with the region's cell count, so at fixed mode prices the condition ranks candidate substrates by their installed energy. In a declared implementation it decides outright, the learning witness of Section~\ref{sec:chain} clearing the line after one update while its matched control stays on the exclusion side at the same conversion factor and diffusion. What is missing for cortex is a price list rather than a mechanism. A mode is a priced rank-one contribution to the kernel, cortical field generation has no canonical decomposition into such modes, and choosing the basis would choose the answer. Applying the scalar threshold to heterogeneous or signed spatial dynamics requires a justified reduction, and identifying the installed kernel with a cortical field is a separate physical commitment.

\section{Compatibility: from coordinated activity to a global description}
\label{sec:unity}

\subsection{Why common timing is insufficient}

For overlapping patches $U$ and $V$, compatibility means that restricting either local description to $U\cap V$ gives the same object. Descriptions can agree on shared quantities while contributing different information elsewhere. Compatible sections of the specified sheaf have a unique global extension. On a finite cover, local finite spatial measures agreeing as measures on every overlap also have a unique finite-measure extension whose sheaf image is the glued section. No mass-one normalization is imposed.

In the cup example, visual and reaching descriptions must express a shared quantity, such as cup location, in common coordinates. Restriction retains that quantity while dropping details particular to each description. Agreement permits surface appearance and hand configuration to contribute to one situation. The mathematical counterexample assigns the same phase to every patch while two local mass profiles disagree on a shared site. Those profiles have no common extension. Because they are chosen independently of phase, synchrony places no constraint on their disagreement.

This distinction limits the biological interpretation. Receptive territories and content domains must be related to spatial patches, and the descriptions must support the required extension. Matching overlap marginals of decoded probability laws need not determine a unique global joint distribution \cite{abramsky2011}.

The cover is itself a choice, and the construction constrains it in one way only: synchronized patches agree where they overlap. Where they do not overlap there is nothing to compare, so a cover by pairwise disjoint patches satisfies that obligation for \emph{every} assignment of local descriptions and phases (\texttt{of\-Disjoint\-Cover}) and the gluing theorem still returns a unique global section, which is the tuple it was handed (\texttt{glue\_\allowbreak unique\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak disjoint}). Membership of the class then reports nothing about the system beyond the patches covering it. What a gluing claim asks of a cover is therefore a non-degeneracy the theorem does not assume: that distinct patches meet (\texttt{Has\-Nonempty\-Overlaps}), and that what they share is a quantity fixed independently of the agreement it is being used to establish.

\subsection{When coherence does constrain content}
\label{sec:content-connection}

A declared relation between phase and content gives a positive result. Let contents take values in a real normed vector space $\mathcal{C}$. Patch $i$ reads an assigned oscillator through $e_{i,x}$ at site $x$, evaluated on the circle point $(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$. Assume a uniform Lipschitz constant $L$ in chord distance and require $e_{i,x}=e_{j,x}$ wherever patches $i$ and $j$ overlap. Encoders may vary across sites; their equality on overlaps is a supplied relation between descriptions of the shared quantity. The order-parameter identity
$N^2r^2=\sum_{ij}\cos(\theta_i-\theta_j)$ implies
$1-\cos(\theta_i-\theta_j)\leq N^2(1-r^2)$ for each pair. Consequently every overlap discrepancy is bounded by
\begin{equation}
 \delta_r=L\sqrt{2}\,N\sqrt{1-r^2}.
 \label{eq:main-content-coherence}
\end{equation}
Here discrepancy is the norm of the difference, and $r$ and $N$ are read on whichever population the bound is applied to. That choice is what decides whether the estimate says anything. A patch carries an order parameter of its own, and reading the bound there carries that patch's resultant and that patch's site count (\texttt{chord\_\allowbreak le\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak patch\_\allowbreak coherence}, \texttt{compatible\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak patch\_\allowbreak coherence}), so a large population observed through small patches is constrained by the small count. Read instead on the whole array, the factor $N$ makes the estimate weak, because it draws a pointwise guarantee from an average over $N^2$ pairs. At $r=1$ the observations agree exactly at either scale. A shared scalar encoder is a special case. Dropping the overlap relation permits disagreement even at perfect locking. A common nonexpansive linear projection preserves agreement, but agreement of a projection does not establish agreement of the full vector.

What Eq.~\eqref{eq:main-content-coherence} bounds is a chord, which never exceeds $2$, so the estimate constrains anything only where $\sqrt{2}\,N\sqrt{1-r^2}$ falls below that. The winding sweep of Section~\ref{sec:wave} fixes both ends of that range on one sheet. Read on the sweep's own patch, \wavePatchSites{} sites at patch-local order \waveBandLocalOrder, the bound is $\wavePatchChordBound\,L$ and constrains nothing; holding that order fixed, the count alone brings it below the maximum chord only at \wavePatchInformativeSites{} sites or fewer. A nearest-neighbour pair of the same winding, the smallest patch there is, gives $\waveNeighbourChordBound\,L$. Shrinking patches therefore thins the overlaps at the same rate as it tightens the bound, and the two ends are not reached by the same cover. At a columnar grain the estimate constrains agreement inside one locally synchronous population and is vacuous across the distant territories whose agreement unity is about.

That vacuity is the uniform bound's, and three results decline the step that puts $N$ there: a sum of $N^2$ nonnegative terms bounded by its total, which permits all the disorder in a population to sit in the pair the conclusion is about. Asking instead how \emph{many} pairs can be far apart is Markov's inequality on the same sum, and its answer carries no population count: at order $r$ the fraction of pairs separated by a chord of $c$ or more is at most $2(1-r^2)/c^2$ at any size of population (\texttt{chord\_\allowbreak fraction\_\allowbreak le}). The same sum read by rows bounds the fraction of \emph{sites} that are far from a fixed fraction of the population (\texttt{site\_\allowbreak fraction\_\allowbreak le}). Read on the patch above, where the uniform bound gives $\wavePatchChordBound\,L$ and constrains nothing, this gives a fraction at most \wavePatchPairFraction{} of pairs disagreeing by more than $L$, and it improves like the square of what the uniform bound improves like. It is a different quantifier and not a stronger statement: a count names no pair, the exceptional set it permits is unlocated and may be exactly the sites a cover's overlaps sit on, and a guarantee about a named overlap therefore still needs the uniform bound at its own grain.

Distance is reached by chaining rather than by counting. Index the cover by its nerve, in which two patches are adjacent when they share a site. The chord is a metric, so a bound $\beta$ on the separation inside every patch composes along a walk: a walk of $k$ hops bounds its endpoints by $(k+1)\beta$ (\texttt{chord\_\allowbreak le\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak patch\_\allowbreak walk\_\allowbreak coherence}). The count is $k+1$ and not $k$ because both endpoint sites pay a step inside their own patch while the shared sites are interior to the chain, and the content statement follows for any cover whose nerve has diameter $k$ (\texttt{compatible\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak patch\_\allowbreak nerve}). No patch need contain both sites, which is the situation of every pair of territories far enough apart, so shrinking patches and spanning territories are two ends of one chain rather than two ends of one bound. What it costs is that the accumulation is linear and nothing makes it sublinear: at the sheet's nearest-neighbour value $\beta=\waveNeighbourChordBound\,L$ the chain stays inside the maximum chord for \waveNerveHops{} hops, which is \waveNerveDiameters{} patch diameters and \waveNerveReachMm~mm of that sheet. The territories whose agreement the compatibility claim is about are separated by centimetres, which is two orders of magnitude beyond where this chain saturates; the route reaches further only on a cover whose patches are larger or more coherent, and both are measurements. Connectivity of the nerve is a property of the cover, supplied rather than derived, and a cover whose nerve is disconnected supports no conclusion between its components.

The third result reads no resultant at all. Where a configuration is frequency-locked to a common rotation $\Omega$, its phase differences are not free: each site's detuning is balanced by its coupling term, $\omega_i-\Omega=\sum_j K_{ij}\sin(\theta_i-\theta_j)$, which is the Kuramoto equations at a rigidly rotating solution rather than a further postulate. Pairing that balance with the configuration itself, symmetrizing, and applying Jordan's inequality termwise turns it into the coupling graph's Dirichlet form, whose algebraic connectivity $\lambda_2$ then bounds the spread about the mean phase $\bar\theta$ by $2\lambda_2\lVert\theta-\bar\theta\rVert\leq\pi\lVert\omega-\Omega\rVert$ (\texttt{spread\_\allowbreak le\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak frequency\_\allowbreak locked}). Read at a named pair it gives a content residual of $L\pi\lVert\omega-\Omega\rVert/(\lambda_2\sqrt{2})$ with no population count, no patch and no cover geometry in it (\texttt{chord\_\allowbreak le\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak frequency\_\allowbreak locked}, \texttt{compatible\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak frequency\_\allowbreak locked}), and it improves as the coupling graph becomes better connected, which is the dependence a resultant cannot express. Where it is informative is fixed by the same hypothesis that licenses it: confinement to a quarter turn already bounds every chord by $\sqrt2$ with no coupling data at all, so the spectral reading adds something exactly where $\lambda_2>\pi\lVert\omega-\Omega\rVert/2$. Four things are declared and none is discharged: $\lambda_2$ is hardware data in the standing of $\kappa$; the locked configuration is assumed, and nothing here proves one exists; the quarter-turn confinement is assumed and is not implied by locking; and the detuning enters in the population's $\ell^2$ norm, so a population whose frequency spread grows with its size pays for that growth through the data. What is removed is the unconditional factor, not the physics of heterogeneity.

Structural connectivity is a declared graph and is what supplies $\lambda_2$. Measured functional connectivity supplies it no better: it is an estimate, so it inherits the decoder failure modes of Section~\ref{sec:observations}, a shared prior shrinking estimates toward agreement and regional bias manufacturing disagreement, and feeding an estimate to a hypothesis strengthens no conclusion drawn from it. Using it to choose the cover is worse than neutral, being the move of Section~\ref{sec:unity} that empties a gluing claim, in different clothes.

Mix retained fields with observations at gain $\eta\in[0,1]$:
$s_i\mapsto(1-\eta)s_i+\eta o_i$.
Content and observation discrepancies bounded by $\varepsilon$ and $\delta$ give a next discrepancy at most $(1-\eta)\varepsilon+\eta\delta$. Repeated observations bounded by $\delta$ therefore give
\begin{equation}
 \varepsilon_n\leq(1-\eta)^n\varepsilon_0+\delta.
 \label{eq:main-content-retention}
\end{equation}
Thus agreeing observations preserve exact compatibility and, for $\eta>0$, reduce an initial mismatch. Approximate observations leave a nondecaying term in the error bound. Taking a uniform bound on Eq.~\eqref{eq:main-content-coherence} connects this update to the phase process.

\subsection{What approximate agreement determines}

Exact gluing requires exact restrictions. For nonnegative profiles on a finite substrate, suppose every overlap discrepancy is at most $\varepsilon$ in uniform distance. Weights that vanish outside their patches and sum to one at each site select a global profile by weighted averaging. The selection lies within $\varepsilon$ of each patch there, and any two such selections differ by at most $\varepsilon$, independently of cover multiplicity. An $L$-Lipschitz readout consequently varies by at most $L\varepsilon$ across selections.

At zero tolerance, the selection is the unique exact extension. At every positive tolerance there are families with no exact extension; selection then gives an approximation whose dependence on weights is bounded. Supplemental Material gives a three-site witness attaining the bound. The mass-profile construction uses the scalar case of the content dynamics and additionally requires nonnegativity of the initial profiles and observations. The general vector result supplies no identification with the spatial-measure sheaf.

These constructions concern declared objects: a specified cover, contents valued in a normed space, and encoders related on overlaps. They select neither the neural cover nor the decoder, and identifying their contents with perceptual content, or establishing that the identification bears on experience, is a further modelling task; Section~\ref{sec:observations} sets out what a measurement of it would have to establish.

\section{Self-representation: a state that models itself}
\label{sec:self}

\subsection{The reflexive construction}

Compatibility gives a global state. Reflexivity relates it to an encoding within that state. Equip global sections with a complete metric. If the additional restriction-resonance condition identifies encoding with restriction to a region $\mathcal{A}$, the reconstruction map is
\begin{equation}
 F(s)=\operatorname{readout}(s|_{\mathcal{A}}).
 \label{eq:selfmap}
\end{equation}
The region and readout are fixed across the state space. If $F$ contracts and fixes the section $s_*$ formed from the local descriptions, Banach's theorem makes $s_*$ its unique fixed point \cite{banach1922}. The formal bridge E89 supplies the contraction and $F(s_*)=s_*$; restriction resonance is separate. Supercritical coupling makes the stipulated contraction factor $\Lambda=e^{-(K-2D)\tau/2}$ less than one for a relaxation time $\tau>0$. Constructing a map with that factor remains a modelling obligation.

In the cup example, the global state includes the represented scene and the perceiver's relation to it. A local encoding must retain enough information for the readout to recover that modelled state; cup position alone would be insufficient if hand configuration and viewpoint could not be reconstructed.

For the same map with Lipschitz factor $\Lambda<1$, approximate reconstruction gives
\begin{equation}
 d(s,F(s))\leq\varepsilon
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 d(s,s_*)\leq\frac{\varepsilon}{1-\Lambda}.
 \label{eq:approximate-self}
\end{equation}
At the stipulated rate this radius grows as $2\varepsilon/((K-2D)\tau)$ near threshold, for fixed positive $\varepsilon$ and $\tau$. Exact uniqueness survives while the error guarantee weakens.

\subsection{Reconstruction across a family of states}
\label{sec:reconstruction-family}

Sensitivity can be required quantitatively. Declare relevant macrostates, an encoder $E$ and readout $R$, and require $d(s,R(E(s)))\leq\varepsilon$ throughout that family. If two states have the same code, the triangle inequality gives
\begin{equation}
 E(s)=E(t)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \max\{d(s,R(E(s))),d(t,R(E(t)))\}\geq\tfrac12 d(s,t).
 \label{eq:reconstruction-separation}
\end{equation}
Thus $M$ relevant states pairwise more than $2\varepsilon$ apart require at least $M$ distinguishable codes; an alphabet of at most $2^b$ codes requires $M\leq2^b$. Applied to the reflexive boundary, $E$ is its local encoding and $R\circ E$ is the existing self-prediction map.

This is an additional accuracy criterion on a family, with no requirement for multiple exact fixed points. A blind encoder or constant readout fails it for separated states despite having a unique fixed point.

Where the encoding is read as the state's own restriction to a region, the criterion becomes a condition on the region. Restriction resonance requires the encoder and that restriction to identify the same pairs of states, so a boundary reconstructing two relevant states more than $2\varepsilon$ apart while its region reads them identically is not resonant (\texttt{not\_\allowbreak isRestrictionResonance\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak reconstructs}). Read forwards, a resonant boundary that reconstructs a pairwise separated family restricts injectively on it (\texttt{restrictToAvatar\_\allowbreak injOn\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak isRestrictionResonance}). Which states a region reads identically is fixed by where the region sits, before an encoding is chosen, so the resolution the family demands is the region's and no readout supplies it. The implication runs one way: a region that does resolve the family is not thereby shown to carry a resonant encoding, and E89 supplies no such resolution.

Those two demands are jointly satisfiable with nothing folded anywhere. Take the region to be the whole substrate: restriction is then the identity, so the boundary is resonant, reconstructs every declarable family exactly, and restricts injectively on all of them (\texttt{top\-Avatar\_\allowbreak satisfies\_\allowbreak obligations}). A properness condition on the region (\texttt{Is\-Proper\-Avatar}) is therefore a separate requirement rather than a consequence of resonance and resolution, and what a fold into a local region is asked for. Its quantitative form replaces the alphabet by a metric. With an $L$-Lipschitz readout, a family reconstructed within $\varepsilon$ whose members lie pairwise more than $d_{\min}$ apart has $\delta$-separated codes whenever $\delta L\leq d_{\min}-2\varepsilon$, so the family is no larger than the $\delta$-packing number of what the region carries (\texttt{encard\_\allowbreak le\_\allowbreak packing\-Number\_\allowbreak restrict\-To\-Avatar}). That is the shape a continuous family of states needs, and at the whole substrate it says nothing, because the region's range is everything. The gain $L$ is declared and derived here for no physical readout, and at unbounded gain the bound says nothing.

\subsection{Agreement after a change takes communication}

Snapshot compatibility does not specify how quickly descriptions respond when a shared quantity changes. In a synchronous network whose updates read only the site's own state and declared neighbours' messages, two initial configurations agreeing on a site's causal past through $T$ rounds produce the same state there at round $T$. The deadline consequence is the one Section~\ref{sec:unconditional} states, and it follows from the reconstruction bound in Section~\ref{sec:reconstruction-family}: a report from that site is a readout of a code that the two interventions leave identical.

A three-site delay line witnesses failure after one round and exact reporting after two; cutting the path prevents delivery at every round. A report may still happen to be correct about one fixed world. The bound concerns responsiveness across interventions.

Throughout this section \emph{prediction} names a reconstruction map, and the relevant family of states, the metric, the tolerance, the communication graph and the deadline are all declared inputs, as are the represented variables and the fidelity counted as accurate. Reading the map as temporal neural prediction would require dynamics; reading the resulting code count as physical memory capacity would require a hardware model, which a state-dependent readout would have to extend.

\section{What the conditions constrain in a digital candidate}
\label{sec:gpu}

The results of Section~\ref{sec:unconditional} hold for any candidate, and for a large language model (LLM) running on graphics processing units (GPUs) four of them are checkable rather than merely stateable. Those four are where this framework bears on claims made for such systems. It does not exclude them, and no result here could; what it does is fix what a claim of unity or self-representation commits its maker to measuring.

\paragraph{Codes, not reports.} Reconstruction across a family is a counting constraint before it is anything else. If two macrostates the candidate is said to represent satisfy $E(s)=E(t)$ while $d(s,t)>2\varepsilon$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:reconstruction-separation} rules out reconstructing both within $\varepsilon$, whatever the readout. A system's reports are readouts. Identical verbal reports across two conditions are therefore consistent with identical internal codes and settle nothing about the states. The converse claim---that the system represents its own condition across a declared family---asserts that its internal code separates that family, which is a statement about activations under intervention and not about text. Naming the channel makes the count concrete. Where the code is the token sequence a model emits---which is what its next step reads---the alphabet is the deployed vocabulary (\texttt{tokenChannel}), and a family reconstructed through it is no larger than the vocabulary size raised to the number of steps (\texttt{card\_\allowbreak le\_\allowbreak card\_\allowbreak tokens}). Over an extended exchange that number is large and the bound slack. At a single step it is one vocabulary's worth, so claiming that a single emission reports the model's own condition across a declared family asserts that the vocabulary separates it (\texttt{card\_\allowbreak le\_\allowbreak card\_\allowbreak tokens\_\allowbreak one}). A claim resting on activations rather than on text has declared a different channel, to which the same count applies with that channel's alphabet. The measurement follows from the statement: declare the family and the fidelity first, then test whether the encoding region distinguishes its members.

\paragraph{The region, not the readout.} Where the encoding is read as the state's restriction to a region of the machine, the demand falls on the region before any readout exists. For a decoder-only transformer the causal mask makes the network an instance of the update model of Section~\ref{sec:self}: the activation at position $t$ and layer $\ell$ reads positions at most $t$ at layer $\ell-1$ and nothing else. A region's causal past is therefore fixed by the architecture rather than chosen by interpretation. That instance is formal, and sharper than slow growth: the causal past of position $t$ stays inside the positions at most $t$ at every depth (\texttt{mask\_\allowbreak ball\_\allowbreak subset\_\allowbreak le}). The deadline bound of Section~\ref{sec:self} therefore fires at every deadline at once rather than at a chosen one, its communication graph given rather than assumed (\texttt{mask\_\allowbreak no\_\allowbreak guarantee}). That is a statement about one forward pass; across steps a model does read its own prior output, which is the channel the preceding paragraph counts. Read against the coupling proposed here the asymmetry runs both ways, and neither way is an advantage. A kernel of full support has no neighbourhood structure for the bound to bite on: after one round the causal past is the whole substrate, so the hypothesis that fires the obstruction is unsatisfiable at any deadline but zero (\texttt{not\_\allowbreak outside\_\allowbreak past\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak isFullSupport}). A physical field propagates at a finite speed, so treating one that way is a calibration against the phase time scale and not a theorem. What the comparison does fix is where the bound has force. Not on the attention graph, where a position reads every position at or before it and the fan-in is the context length. On the physical interconnect, fan-in is genuinely bounded: a graph whose sites each hear from at most $\nu$ others reaches at most $(T+1)\nu^{T}$ sites in $T$ rounds (\texttt{card\_\allowbreak ball\_\allowbreak le\_\allowbreak mul\_\allowbreak pow}), so a model sharded across many devices pays a number of rounds growing like $\log_{\nu}$ of the device count. Buying that deadline back costs wiring: reaching every site in one round requires a device hearing from all but one of the others (\texttt{le\_\allowbreak degree\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak reaches\_\allowbreak one}). Both $\nu$ and the device count are declared, as the communication graph and the deadline already are, and what the count bounds is the graph a guarantee requires rather than what hardware is buildable. A claim that a model reports on its own processing consequently carries an obligation to say which positions and layers the report's causal past contains. A report that appears to track an intervention outside it indicates a confound or a different region, not a faster channel.

\paragraph{Agreement about what.} Unity requires the candidate's local descriptions to agree on independently identified shared quantities (Section~\ref{sec:unity}). Common timing, a shared residual stream and fluent output supply none of that, for the reason Section~\ref{sec:unconditional} gives: coordination bounds content disagreement only through encoders already related on overlaps. Which quantities two parts of a model describe in common is a decoding question, carrying the failure modes of Section~\ref{sec:observations}. The cover is a choice, and choosing it to secure agreement empties the claim.

\paragraph{What is not excluded.} Digital representations can satisfy compatibility, so finite hardware is compatible with the gluing construction and none of the above is a no-go for machines. The coupling-gated route E56--E78 is a different matter: it requires a calibrated physical coupling, its noise regime and a connection from coordination to content agreement, and an attention matrix or a shared clock supplies none of these identifications. Computing a field model likewise leaves its realization in the computing substrate to be established. Those physical obligations fall on the cortical proposal identically. The capacity comparison those support comparisons lack is available, and it favours neither side by construction. A continuum code space read at resolution $\delta$ holds at least its volume divided by the volume of a resolution cell, so it out-resolves an alphabet of $2^b$ codes exactly when that volume exceeds $2^b$ cells (\texttt{two\_\allowbreak pow\_\allowbreak lt\_\allowbreak packingNumber\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak lt\_\allowbreak measure}). Below that threshold the inequality runs the other way, and at a coarse enough resolution the region holds a single code. What decides the comparison is the volume, the resolution and the dimension, all measured quantities, and continuity decides nothing on its own: a continuum code space read at a finite resolution is a finite alphabet whose capacity is a bit count. For the same reason this account credits an analog medium with no computation beyond a Turing machine's. Real-valued states carry unbounded capacity only at unbounded precision, and the noise floor $D>0$ that Eq.~\eqref{eq:kuramoto} is stated against removes it.

The installed-energy condition of Section~\ref{sec:installation} is restricted in the same way. It constrains a macroscopic candidate only through $\kappa$, which is hardware data supplied by mode profiles and prices, and Supplemental Material reads the condition against a cortical metabolic budget to return the conversion factor it would require rather than a verdict. The hardware comparisons there have similarly restricted scope: fixed wiring can impose a correlation disadvantage, and point-supported kernels vanish under an atomless continuum measure, but neither comparison makes the representation conditions unattainable on GPUs. A universal exclusion would need a necessary condition for consciousness and a proof that every relevant implementation violates it, and the present composition supplies neither.

Whether experience requires a living substrate is the explicit subject of biological naturalism \cite{seth2026,seth2026response}. On that view the properties grounding experience are those of self-sustaining organisms, and simulating them does not secure them. The conditions stated here name no biological mechanism, but computation described as such does not satisfy them either: a candidate must exhibit a physically realized coupling in a measured noise regime, agreement about independently identified shared quantities, and an internal reconstruction meeting a declared fidelity. Whether a realization of those conditions that is not alive would be accompanied by experience is the point at issue, and these results do not settle it. A related position locates the difference in the mode of computation rather than in life as such: biological systems compute across scales that do not separate, and alongside discrete operations carry continuous-valued dynamics belonging to their physical medium \cite{milinkovic2026}. The coupling condition stated here is one measurable instance of that requirement, fixed to a physical field in a declared noise regime, and a description of such a field does not satisfy it.

The neuroscientific case against \emph{human-like} consciousness in text-only LLMs concerns embodied sensory interaction, thalamocortical organization and biological regulation linking perception and action to survival \cite{aru2023}. Their absence supports skepticism, conditional on their causal relevance to experience. Those mechanisms are not requirements of the abstract gluing and reconstruction definitions, and workspace-like representations reported in some LLMs \cite{gurnee2026} supply candidate organization to investigate. What this framework adds to that investigation is an order for the questions: declare the family of states and the fidelity, fix the encoding region and read its causal past off the architecture, and only then ask what the system reconstructs. A claim that survives those three steps is a claim about the machine; one that does not survive them was a claim about its output.

\section{Thermodynamics and composition}
\label{sec:chain}

The phase and representation results concern coupling, content disagreement and reconstruction error. Thermodynamics constrains the physical operations that could implement them, and it does so from the side rather than along the spine: no edge below derives coherence, compatibility or reconstruction from a heat bound, and the resource condition these edges terminate in is necessary without being restrictive for any macroscopic candidate (Section~\ref{sec:installation}). What this half contributes is a ledger in which a resource claim is stated in checkable units. Finite-register capacity obeys $H(p)\leq\log|X|$; erasure heat depends on specified system--bath assumptions \cite{landauer1961,norton2005,shenker2000}. A separate boundary result concerns a field reaching disconnected vacuum components \cite{kibble1976}. The composition connects these objects through the explicit hypotheses in Table~\ref{tab:summary}.

\subsection{Feedback changes the information constraint}

For passive memory $X_t$ driven by an environmental signal $S_t$, the predictive bound is
\begin{equation}
 W_{\mathrm{diss}}\geq k_BT\left[I(X_t;S_t)-I(X_t;S_{t+1})\right].
 \label{eq:predictive}
\end{equation}
The Markov structure $X_t\to S_t\to S_{t+1}$ makes the bracket nonnegative \cite{still2012}. Zero retained information also satisfies it, so a task and a learning rule require additional specification. Relating this bound to Landauer heat requires a common thermal scale and budget, and memory waste bounded by erased entropy.

An acting agent changes the signal it will receive. Specify policy, world, observation and memory-update channels. For their finite laws,
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
 I(X_t;S_t)-I(X_t;S_{t+1})
 ={}&I(X_t;S_t\mid S_{t+1})\\
 &-I(X_t;S_{t+1}\mid S_t).
\end{split}
\label{eq:feedback-information}
\end{equation}
The second conditional term vanishes in the passive model. With feedback, the signed difference can be negative as action creates correlations with the future. A causal reading requires an environmental state sufficient to describe the relevant dynamics.

For a controlled substep, the active thermodynamic bound instead uses the joint entropy. Forward and reverse path laws give
$\Sigma=D_{KL}(P_F\|P_R)=\Delta H(X,S)+\langle Q\rangle/(k_BT)\geq0$
under local detailed balance, with thermal scale $k_BT>0$ and heat positive into the reservoir \cite{horowitz2014,ito2013}. Every forward path of positive probability must have a positive-probability reverse for this finite identity. A missing reverse gives infinite divergence. The supplement develops the support conditions and composes actuation with memory updates on their shared intermediate law.

\subsection{Learning and repeated operation need explicit resources}

The finite examples distinguish selecting a feasible policy, relaxing a register toward an encoded reward, and learning an unknown task from observations. The observational learner acquires correlation with an environmental parameter while its action marginal stays uniform. A continuing model executes actuation, observation-driven learning and reset in sequence, preserving above-chance performance at a positive work cost per cycle.

A phase-derived sensor makes the informational distinction concrete: an informative readout and a blind control have the same phase order but different learning performance. Attaching the learner's register to the actuator of Section~\ref{sec:installation} ties reward, installed kernel and work to its own executed law. The learning witness rises above the necessary installed-energy bound while its matched control remains on the exclusion line. These finite constructions, detailed in Supplemental Material, leave the evolving feedback between phase dynamics, installation and sensing to be specified.

For that model, common energy observable $\mathcal{E}$, thermal scale $k_BT$, local detailed balance and positive support at each stage give
\begin{equation}
 k_BT[H(p_0)-H(p_N)]\leq Q_{<N}
 =W_{<N}+\langle \mathcal{E}\rangle_0-\langle \mathcal{E}\rangle_N
 \leq B+\langle \mathcal{E}\rangle_0-\langle \mathcal{E}\rangle_N,
 \label{eq:continuing-budget}
\end{equation}
where expected cumulative work stays within the supplied allowance $B$ at every prefix. This bounds expectations. Separate constructions track a store on each trajectory, include finite sources, charge sensor clearing and prepend preparation. They specify when a mean allowance represents an available resource; an expected budget alone permits overdrawn paths.

These constructions establish costs for their declared processes. A finite source with a positive net cost permits only finitely many funded cycles, while continuing operation requires replenishment. Register--bath witnesses also distinguish actual bath-energy transfer from transition-log heat. Identifying a work store, an erasure budget and the installation energy of Section~\ref{sec:installation} requires physical relations that these separate models do not supply automatically.

\subsection{What the composition establishes}

The passive theorem \texttt{chain} and feedback theorem \texttt{chain\_\allowbreak active} each accept eight connecting implications (Table~\ref{tab:summary}). In the active branch, a named substep at the register's thermal scale satisfies
\begin{equation}
 k_BT>0,\qquad k_BT[H(p)-H(p')]\leq\langle Q\rangle\leq B,
 \label{eq:active-chain-bound}
\end{equation}
with $B$ the register's heat. The upper allocation follows from a ledger whose operations deliver that total heat to one reservoir and whose other operations do not increase joint entropy. Those ledger assumptions are physical inputs. Neither Landauer's lower bound nor microscopic reversibility supplies them. The regime in which those premises are exhibited is a near-equilibrium one: strictly positive forward and reverse elementary paths, local detailed balance at one thermal scale, and entropy differences and heats of order $k_BT$, the witnesses being two-bit systems with interaction energies of that size. Cortex is not in it. The installed energy of Section~\ref{sec:installation} is $\energyThermalQuanta$ times $kT$ at body temperature over one region and one phase-diffusion time, and a budget $B$ of that size stands against an entropy term of order $k_BT$, so Eq.~\eqref{eq:active-chain-bound} is satisfied by any macroscopic candidate and excludes none of them. Reading the register instead at the scale where its premises are physical, such as a single channel, relocates the difficulty to E45Active, which would then have to carry one molecular step's budget to the convergence of a continuum kernel. The active branch is an alternative route to the same node rather than a stronger one, and no result of Section~\ref{sec:installation} passes through it: the installed-energy condition is derived from E56 and E67.

\section{What the observations establish}
\label{sec:observations}

The composition holds under its premises. Whether observation can establish those premises is a separate question, and the three requirements it connects concern different objects: phase dynamics, local descriptions and internal encodings. Their observables must retain these distinctions. The following controls show which mechanisms remain indistinguishable, which temporal approximations fail, which states one observable cannot separate, and what independent content measurements must establish. Each is a finite numerical regime on a declared model, with full parameters and controls in Supplemental Material, and none of them calibrates a cortical quantity.

\subsection{Stationary distributions leave the mechanism undetermined}
\label{sec:identifiability}

Equation~\eqref{eq:selfconsistency} gives a concentration--coherence relation with no fitted shape parameter. Estimate $r$ from circular resultant length and $a$ from density shape: inverting $R$ from the measured $r$ would make agreement automatic. Even independently estimated agreement identifies a statistical family shared by different mechanisms.

Let $q(t)$ cross $2$, let $r(q)$ be its stationary branch, and fix $D_0>0$. Increasing coupling $K(t)=D_0q(t)$ at diffusion $D_0$ and decreasing diffusion $D(t)=K_0/q(t)$ at coupling $K_0$ both give concentration $a=q(t)r(q(t))$. Uncoupled oscillators with prescribed drift $-h(t)\sin(\theta-\psi)$, where $h(t)=D_0q(t)r(q(t))$, and diffusion $D_0$ have that same density. Their stationary distributions coincide window by window despite different causes.

Resolved dynamics supply additional information. In the ideal model with continuous paths, quadratic variation accumulates at rate $2D$ and conditional increment means constrain drift. Separating endogenous coupling from a matched common drive additionally requires a measured drive or controlled perturbation; identifying electromagnetic coupling requires evidence specific to that interaction. Supplemental Material gives the equivalence construction and its numerical check.

\subsection{Finite ramps change the observable onset}
\label{sec:ramp}

The square-root law in Eq.~\eqref{eq:onset} describes stationary solutions. A changing coupling requires time for the phase distribution to approach those solutions. In a deterministic mean-field control that crosses the threshold symmetrically, none of the scanned rates meets the declared tracking tolerance: slowing the crossing does not remove the terminal residual under that initialization. A slow control variable therefore supplies no general guarantee of branch tracking at onset. Supplemental Material gives the residual, rate range and admissible rates away from threshold.

The finite-population ramp study uses $N=2000$, $D=1$ and 32 replicas at $\rampDelaySpeedCount$ coupling speeds from $v=\rampDelaySpeedFastest$ to $v=\rampDelaySpeedSlowest$. Escape is the first of three consecutive saved samples at $r\geq0.2$ after threshold. Faster ramps delay escape (Fig.~\ref{fig:ramp}); only $\rampFastEscaped/\rampReplicas$ replicas escape within the fastest window. The $\rampDelayUncensoredCount$ uncensored legs supply the delay scaling below; the decade-spaced ones among them have mean coupling delays $\rampDelaySlowOne$, $\rampDelaySlowTwo$ and $\rampDelaySlowThree$. Fitted onset exponents differ from those of the exact stationary branch evaluated on the same grids. At the fastest speed the ensemble mean never reaches the fit window, so its fitted slope does not estimate stationary onset.

Delayed passage through a noisy bifurcation \cite{berglund2002} makes the apparent exponent depend on ramp speed and fitting window. The specified deviation-based critical ramp speed is not bracketed by this sweep. With fluctuating coupling, the relevant stationary comparison also depends on rate: the mean coupling approximates order in the tested fast regime, while averaging stationary order over coupling works for the tested slow drives confined to one side of threshold. Slow crossing drives can satisfy neither approximation.

\paragraph{The prediction.} The delay scaling is what this section supports as a prediction, and it needs no absolute calibration. Over the $\rampDelayUncensoredCount$ uncensored legs the mean delay follows $\Delta K\propto v^{\rampDelayExponent}$, with a $95\%$ slope interval of $[\rampDelayExponentLow,\rampDelayExponentHigh]$, which does not contain $1/2$. What that shortfall measures is the escape criterion and not the scaling. Applied to a deterministic mean-field run seeded at the fluctuation floor, the same estimator returns $\quasistaticDelayExponent$ over the same legs at the same escape criterion, inside the interval on the measurement. That run has no noise to delay it, so the shortfall is the criterion's: it crosses the saturation between the floor it starts from and the level it is stopped at, and tightening the criterion to $r\geq\quasistaticEscapeTight$ returns $\quasistaticEscapeTightExponent$. The bias is reproduced where the answer is known, which is what licenses reading the measurement as the law rather than as a competing exponent.

Stated as a law rather than as a number: onset delay scales as the square root of the rate at which coupling crosses threshold. Testing it requires only that $\dot K$ be monotonic in a manipulated rate---not its value, nor the installation factor $\kappa$, nor the field-to-coupling conversion rate $\gamma$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:keff}. It is therefore a within-subject design varying the rate of emergence from anaesthesia alone, each participant serving as their own control. Such a design must avoid the hazard this sweep exhibits: a fixed absolute escape criterion meets a size-dependent critical fluctuation floor, so the criterion has to be referred to each recording's own pre-threshold fluctuation scale rather than to a shared number. The diffusion $D$ is the remaining input, and Section~\ref{sec:identifiability} notes that it is estimable from resolved phase traces through the rate $2D$ at which quadratic variation accumulates, which is a different quantity from the concentration the propofol exercise could not range over.

\subsection{A winding state leaves the order parameter ambiguous}
\label{sec:wave}

The order parameter $r$ is indifferent to where a phase sits in space. A field winding once across a periodic sheet therefore has $r=0$ while every patch of it stays locked. Such a field is not an artificial construction here. On a torus a winding is stationary for any isotropic symmetric kernel at identical frequencies: the coupling term is odd in the separation against a kernel even in it, so the drift cancels pair by pair. \texttt{char\_\allowbreak is\_\allowbreak kuramoto\_\allowbreak trajectory} proves this for any finite periodic array, this sheet included. A winding satisfies the positivity and identical-frequency hypotheses. It violates only the quarter-turn initial spread that the convergence result additionally requires before placing the limit at the uniform state.

On the sheet of Section~\ref{sec:field}'s spatial control, a winding survives below an interpolated decay length of \waveBoundary~mm and unwinds above it (Fig.~\ref{fig:winding}). That boundary is interior to the 0.1--0.3~mm band. At 0.1~mm the same sheet at the same coupling, diffusion and kernel reaches $r=\waveBandControlOrder$ from a uniform start and $r=\waveBandOrder$ from a winding one. Both are stationary, with patch-local order \waveBandLocalOrder{} and no winding defect. At fixed $K$ the observable therefore takes two values spanning nearly its whole range. Coupling does not determine the resultant, and a low resultant does not by itself distinguish weak coupling from a winding field.

\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.96\linewidth]{figures/travelling_wave/travelling_wave_sweep.png}
\caption{A winding phase field on the spatial sheet, at identical frequencies. Left: global order (solid) against patch-local order (dashed) across a subset of coupling decay lengths. Right: the twisted and uniform initial conditions compared across lengths, with the shaded 0.1--0.3~mm band and the interpolated retention boundary. Below that boundary the two initial conditions reach different stationary values of $r$ at the same coupling, diffusion and kernel, so the resultant does not report the coupling. \label{fig:winding}
\end{figure}

Under the quenched frequency spread of \waveSpread~rad\,s$^{-1}$ that the spatial control carries, the boundary moves to \waveSpreadBoundary~mm. The same comparison at 0.1~mm gives $\waveSpreadBandControlOrder$ against $\waveSpreadBandOrder$, holding across \waveSeedCount{} frozen realizations. Heterogeneity shortens the range that sustains a wave without removing it.

Which windings persist has a criterion behind it. Linearizing the phase model about a winding returns a coupling of the same translation-invariant form, each separation reweighted by the cosine of the phase the winding advances across it (\texttt{hasDerivAt\_\allowbreak charJacobian}). That reweighted kernel is again even in the separation, so the same characters diagonalize it and every mode's rate is one of its eigenvalues. Where the winding advances by at most a quarter turn across every separation the coupling reaches, the reweighted kernel is nonnegative and no rate is positive (\texttt{charJacobian\_\allowbreak stable\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak quarter\_\allowbreak turn}); where some rate is positive, that mode grows without bound (\texttt{charJacobian\_\allowbreak growing\_\allowbreak mode}). Moving coupling mass to longer separations is what carries a phase advance past a quarter turn, and that is the mechanism the decay-length sweep varies. The criterion bounds rates rather than trajectories: asymptotic stability is not proved, so the states that persist occupy a narrower range than it admits, and the boundary quoted above is measured and not derived from it. The same kernel is the potential's curvature, so a winding at $r=0$ is not the global minimum the convergence result places its limit at, and one carrying a growing mode is not a local minimum either: that result's quarter-turn hypothesis must exclude a winding rather than merely decline to cover it (\texttt{char\_\allowbreak not\_\allowbreak potential\_\allowbreak min}).

An amplitude layer decides which of these states exist, and leaves what the resultant reports unchanged. Every oscillator of the phase model sits on the unit circle, so a field with an amplitude node has no representation in those dynamics. The real Stuart--Landau extension supplies one on the same coupling, and leaves intact the gradient structure the convergence results rest on. The state becomes $z_i\in\mathbb{C}$ with $\dot z_i=(\Gamma-|z_i|^2)z_i+\sum_j A_{ij}(z_j-z_i)$. Its phase equation is the Kuramoto equation with each coupling weighted by a ratio of amplitudes (\texttt{amplitude\_\allowbreak phase\_\allowbreak is\_\allowbreak kuramoto}). The phase model is this one with the amplitude ratios set to one, rather than a separate object.

What the added degree of freedom decides is which windings exist at all. On a periodic array of any dimension, this sheet included, a winding requires $\Gamma+\lambda>0$, with $\Gamma$ the layer's linear gain. Here $\lambda=\sum_d f_d(\cos\Delta\theta_d-1)$ is the eigenvalue the kernel supplies, summed over separations, with $\Delta\theta_d$ the phase the winding advances across $d$ (\texttt{exists\_\allowbreak charState\_\allowbreak iff}; on the ring $\Delta\theta_d=2\pi qd/n$). For the nonnegative kernels used here $\lambda\leq0$, and moving coupling mass to longer separations lowers it further. A broad enough kernel therefore removes the winding outright. The sweep above reaches that same boundary by a different route: the phase model retains every winding at every coupling, and loses this one under integration instead. The two layers bound the same regime from opposite sides. A phase recording does not separate a state the amplitude layer excludes from one the dynamics fails to sustain.

Three limits bound the layer for a reader of the cortical literature. These states are windings and not spirals, and on the sheet that is a theorem rather than a shortage of dimensions. Equal modulus at every site puts the phase circulation at exactly zero on every plaquette, and on a periodic sheet the circulations of \emph{any} field sum to zero, so a lone defect is not a state of this geometry at all. Relaxational dynamics has no propagation speed, which is the quantity a cortical wave measurement leads with. And $|z|$ is a normal-form radius, which a measured envelope is not; this work identifies the two nowhere. Supplemental Material gives the sweep, its uniform control, the amplitude layer, the standing wave it admits inside the band, the potential's curvature at a winding and the defect theorems, together with the remaining limits of both.

A vanishing resultant is the absence of global agreement and not of agreement. Eq.~\eqref{eq:main-content-coherence} read on a nearest-neighbour patch of a winding gives a discrepancy bound of $2\sqrt{2}\,L\,|\sin\Delta\theta|$, with $\Delta\theta$ the phase advanced across the patch, which falls to zero as the same winding is spread over more sites (\texttt{chord\_\allowbreak le\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak char\_\allowbreak patch}), while the global form of the same bound is vacuous at $r=0$. Encoders shared on overlaps therefore still bound content disagreement inside a patch of a state that no global observable calls coherent. The winding number itself stays unconnected to content. It is, however, a property of the state rather than of the cover read on it. On a ring the whole turns dropped by the winding's affine local lifts are zero at every site but the wrap, where the residue resets, and they sum around the loop to $q$; a nonzero $q$ obstructs every global real-valued phase lift, so one state at one coupling has resultant exactly zero and degree $q$ (\texttt{winding\_\allowbreak degree\_\allowbreak obstructs}). Reweighting the sites moves the first quantity and redrawing the loop's intermediate transitions leaves the second where it was. The scope is the ring's: this is a loop's winding and not a spiral, the transitions are those of the affine lifts rather than of an unwrapped measurement, and the integer is identified with no cortical observable.

This qualifies what a phase trace can establish. An onset criterion stated in $r$ alone registers a change of basin as readily as a change of coupling. The protocol of Section~\ref{sec:protocol} therefore needs a spatial observable beside the resultant.

\subsection{Coherent adaptation can lose environmental alignment}
\label{sec:plasticity}

An adaptive coupling rule might supply representational structure, but its objective must reward the relevant agreement. For symmetric coupling, pairwise sine terms cancel in the population mean drift. Minimizing squared drift at that fixed mean therefore rewards equalizing drift across nodes. In the frequency-cluster control, this favors coupling between unlike-frequency clusters, whereas the diagnostic environmental template rewards coupling within clusters.

The formalization proves descent of the squared-drift objective for a specified gradient flow at fixed phases. Its functional differs from thermodynamic entropy production: at a coherent stationary density the probability current vanishes while phase-averaged squared drift is positive. Supplemental Material gives the operator, its gradient and the conditions of the descent result.

The finite control co-evolves noisy phases and symmetric, nonnegative couplings at fixed total resource. Its template is a diagnostic covariance of latent signals, never a training input. Across three seeds, second-half order remains between $\plasticityOrderMin$ and $\plasticityOrderMax$, while the within-cluster to between-cluster coupling ratio falls from $\plasticityRatioInitialMin$--$\plasticityRatioInitialMax$ to $\plasticityRatioMin$--$\plasticityRatioMax$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:resonance}). Random-update, permuted-gradient and frozen-kernel controls are reported in Supplemental Material.

The immediate post-update objective reduction disappears when averaged over complete update intervals. A prespecified grid of $\plasticityStudyCaseCount$ rate--cadence combinations contains no case meeting the $5\%$ sustained-reduction criterion while retaining coherence and losing alignment. The finding is coherent adaptation away from the specified structure, with no demonstrated sustained cost reduction.

Kernel alignment and overlap compatibility are different observables. This control addresses acquisition of its designated template under its particular objective. Learning models can explicitly include data in an objective \cite{corberi2026}; an account of agreement about the cup must specify the content its learning rule is meant to preserve.

\subsection{Content agreement needs independent decoders}

A neural test needs independently identified domains, local descriptions and restriction maps. Two decoders should estimate a shared content on overlapping domains before their agreement is examined. Discrepancy requires a null under decoding noise and controls using deliberately compatible and incompatible examples. An implementation on constructed data separates these cases at $\mathrm{AUC}=\overlapIncompatibleAuc$, on a $95\%$ interval of $[\overlapIncompatibleAucLow,\overlapIncompatibleAucHigh]$ resampled by trial; the compatible construction scores $\overlapCompatibleAuc$ on an interval containing one half. Shared-prior shrinkage can nevertheless manufacture agreement, and regional bias can manufacture disagreement. Per-patch decoding diagnostics detect these failures; the compatibility statistic alone does not.

Phase agreement cannot replace that measurement. On constructed content, phase carries a share \overlapPhaseShareBuilt{} of the content entropy when content is built from it and \overlapPhaseShareFree{} when it is not. A phase-derived statistic reports compatibility on phase-locked patches whose local descriptions disagree. The implemented content statistic detects their incompatibility. These controls validate a measurement on known constructions; a neural content model and decoder remain to be established.

Choosing the cover also chooses a candidate subject: trivial descriptions can agree while carrying little content, and a partition can conceal disagreement. The useful choice must therefore be constrained by what neural activity encodes. The exact composition assumes compatibility of its supplied cover. The content mechanisms above identify additional hypotheses under which agreement can arise; they leave the empirical choice of content and the connection to that cover explicit.

\subsection{What an informative phase-density measurement requires}

The concentration range and the dependence structure decide whether the concentration--coherence relation can be tested at all. Under the specified log-density estimator and bias-floor criterion, discrimination at the observed concentration ceiling $a=\designObservedMax$ requires \designSitesIndependent{} independent sites in the scanned designs, rising to \designSitesClustered{} when sites share a mean in groups of \designSitesPerCluster. These counts describe that estimator and dependence model; the intended recording requires its own calibration. Supplemental Material reports the bin-count controls and the full dependence-aware design.

That ceiling comes from exercising the estimators on public propofol-sedation EEG \cite{bajwa2025}, pooling channels in 100-ms windows. Bipolar and common-reference montages produce different residuals. The bipolar results are compatible with the Bessel relation over a concentration range too narrow to distinguish it from its linear approximation at the observed scatter, which is the range the design scan takes as given. Temporal pooling changes the estimand, and sedation recordings leave the awakening hypothesis untested.

An informative dataset therefore needs sufficient concentration range or precision, calibrated estimators and an observation model for dependence, source mixing and reference choice. Public mouse calcium imaging provides thousands of spatially located neurons across waking, sleep and anesthesia \cite{oomoto2026}, but its 7.65\,Hz frame rate is below the phase-estimation band used here. Site count, state coverage and bandwidth are separate requirements.

\section{The cortical hypothesis and an awakening test}
\label{sec:prediction}

The cortical proposal identifies a physical source of coupling and asks whether its changes help explain recovery. The tests in Section~\ref{sec:observations} determine what observations must distinguish before a phase trace can support this identification.

\subsection{Physical identification and calibration}
\label{sec:scaling}

The cortical hypothesis identifies part of effective coupling with endogenous extracellular electromagnetic interactions. Fields can modulate spike timing \cite{frohlich2010,anastassiou2015}, and field models propose coordination of distributed neural information \cite{pinotsis2022,mcfadden2020}. Phase organization under propofol motivates studying this regime \cite{bastos2021}, but does not measure coupling.

The formal field predicate requires a jointly continuous kernel, a normalized substrate, coupling equal to the kernel average, and no single site providing more than half that strength. Toy constructions satisfy it. A cortical realization must specify the scale at which the kernel and its reduction apply: calcium imaging reports different state-dependent modularity patterns at single-cell and mesoscale resolution \cite{kiyooka2026}.

Cortical columns are the obvious candidate unit, and they answer one of the two demands the formalism makes. As the oscillator index they are a serious proposal: they are delineated by response properties and anatomy rather than by the agreement they would be used to establish \cite{mountcastle1997}, and a phase can be estimated from a column-sized territory at the electrode spacings used for cortical wave measurements \cite{townsend2015}. As the content cover they are a poor one, for three separate reasons. A parcellation into non-overlapping columns is a disjoint cover, which satisfies the compatibility obligation without constraining anything (Section~\ref{sec:unity}). The cover condition additionally requires the patches to exhaust the substrate, while much of association cortex has no accepted columnar parcellation. And the column's standing as a canonical cortical unit is contested \cite{horton2005,rakic2008}. Offering columns for both roles at once therefore supplies the oscillator index and leaves the content cover to be identified on separate evidence, which is the bridge assumption E78 records.

The spatial reduction is also testable. A row-sum aggregation at zero diagonal tracks the uniform-coupling control over the tested spatial decay range, while frequency heterogeneity displaces the operational threshold. The fit window biases both estimates relative to $2D$; agreement with the control is therefore the supported conclusion. Supplemental Material reports the reduction, its numerical error and the distinct assumptions needed for the kernel-average identification in Eq.~\eqref{eq:installed-coupling-bound}.

The field commitment requires physical evidence beyond a choice of mathematical representation, and the available support comparisons do not supply it. A finite matrix spread over positive-mass cells preserves its total coupling and phase correlation exactly, while point-supported vanishing under an atomless measure and a disadvantage from excluding a better-correlated pair concern specified support constraints; Section~\ref{sec:gpu} reads the same comparisons against a digital candidate. Neither supplies energy prices.

A spike-time shift alone supplies no coupling rate. The dimensionless product of population size $N$, timing sensitivity $\mathcal{T}$, field amplitude $E$ and frequency $f$ needs a conversion rate $\gamma$:
\begin{equation}
 K_{\mathrm{eff}}=\gamma N \mathcal{T} E f.
 \label{eq:keff}
\end{equation}
This rate describes how induced timing shifts accumulate into drift. Neither it nor the installation factor $\kappa$ follows from a heat bound. Testing the physical threshold requires independent estimates of the interaction kernel, its aggregation and diffusion in the same cortical regime.

\subsection{Extracellular geometry and recovery}

The proposed control variable is extracellular geometry. Extracellular volume fraction is approximately $\alpha=0.2$ at tortuosity $\lambda\approx1.6$ \cite{sykova2008}; a sleep study reports a rise from roughly $0.14$ to $0.23$ \cite{xie2013}. Below $10$\,kHz, cortical tissue conducts approximately resistively at $0.3$--$0.6$\,S\,m$^{-1}$ \cite{barbour2017}. At fixed transmembrane current density, an effective-medium description predicts that a larger extracellular fraction raises conductivity and reduces field amplitude.

The coupling-gated hypothesis consequently predicts that extracellular contraction on awakening raises $E$ and, holding the other factors in Eq.~\eqref{eq:keff} fixed, raises effective coupling. This argument supplies a direction; the geometry--coupling relation, its magnitude and its role in human recovery require measurement.

For a smooth crossing at nonzero speed, evaluating the stationary branch along the changing control gives the benchmark
\begin{equation}
 r_{\mathrm{ss}}(K(t))\sim\sqrt{\frac{\dot K(t_0)}{D}}\,(t-t_0)^{1/2},
 \qquad K(t_0)=K_c,\quad \dot K(t_0)>0.
 \label{eq:recovery}
\end{equation}
This follows from Eq.~\eqref{eq:onset} at fixed $D$ and concerns the stationary branch evaluated at $K(t)$. Reading it as observed recovery requires an independently justified tracking approximation. The crossing controls in Section~\ref{sec:ramp} leave that approximation unsupported at onset, even though a separation of geometry and phase time scales can support it away from threshold. The awakening hypothesis therefore needs a calibrated dynamical prediction for an operational onset above the finite-size fluctuation floor. Crossing $K_c$ alone does not supply a recovery latency.

\subsection{A protocol for the awakening experiment}
\label{sec:protocol}

The physical test has six requirements:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Coupling and diffusion in the same units.} Estimate them from resolved phase dynamics, separating heterogeneity and observation noise. Measure the drive or perturb the system to distinguish endogenous coupling.
\item \textbf{Geometry calibrated to coupling.} Validate the geometry observable and its relation to effective coupling, with uncertainty. The conductivity argument supplies only the expected direction.
\item \textbf{Calibrated phase measurements.} Estimate $r$ by resultant length and $a$ by unweighted regression of log density on centered cosine. Choose site count and binning from a dependence-aware calibration over the intended concentration range. The tested designs require thousands of sites at the observed concentrations; temporal pooling changes the spatial estimand.
\item \textbf{An independent recovery endpoint.} Measure behavioural recovery separately from phase organization.
\item \textbf{A predeclared dynamical onset criterion.} Set a coherence threshold above the finite-size floor and evaluate its latency under the measured coupling trajectory, including ramp speed or fluctuations. Record a spatial observable beside the resultant, since a winding field reports a low resultant at coupling that sustains local order (Section~\ref{sec:wave}).
\item \textbf{Matched model comparison.} Compare coupling increase, diffusion change, common drive and alternative recovery models under the same observation and filtering assumptions.
\end{enumerate}

Failure of the concentration--coherence relation over an informative range constrains the density model. Failure of the calibrated geometry--coupling association constrains the physical gate. Better prediction of independent recovery by a competing mechanism counts against its proposed role. A favorable outcome still leaves the content of coordinated activity to be explained.

\section{Discussion}

Two things have been established here, and they are of different kinds. The constraints of Section~\ref{sec:unconditional} hold outright: synchrony does not deliver compatible content, and where shared encoders let it bound content at all, the bound that holds uniformly over pairs does so only inside patches too small to span the territories at issue, and distance costs either a connected cover or a locked configuration with a declared spectral gap. The resultant does not determine the state; a unique fixed point is not a reconstruction; a region that cannot resolve a family cannot encode it; and agreement about a change takes communication. Any account that reads unity off phase coherence has to meet them, this one included. The account set out in Section~\ref{sec:intro} is conditional at every step by contrast: each connection in it is a hypothesis supplied rather than a result derived, and Table~\ref{tab:summary} names all eight. Its four non-standard ingredients, named in Section~\ref{sec:intro}, are the installed-energy condition, the patch-local form of the content bound, the criterion separating a winding field from weak coupling, and the machine-checked composition with its joint witness. What follows takes the conditions in turn, as commitments the proposal incurs, as measurements that would bear on them, and as questions the framework leaves open.

\subsection{Unity and a minimal self as empirical commitments}
\label{sec:commitment}

We propose the state glued from compatible local descriptions as a correlate of conscious unity, and its accurate internal reconstruction as a correlate of a minimal self. The motivations are that different contents belong to one experienced situation and that the situation is experienced from a perspective. The conditional results identify relations a realization would need: sufficient installed resources for the specified phase model, agreement about shared content, and an encoding that supports reconstruction at the required fidelity. Discharging E12--E89 for cortex would make the composition a statement about cortex: that a cortical field above threshold relaxes to a cover whose compatible local descriptions glue, and that the glued state is the unique fixed point of a reconstruction map realized there. It would leave the two identifications above exactly where they are, because neither is among the eight and neither follows from their conjunction; they are the commitment this subsection names, and Section~\ref{sec:full-test} lists the three observations that would count against it.

Compatibility allows different descriptions to contribute different information. It also permits uncertainty and conflicting judgments: apparent cup position and judged cup position can be distinct quantities in a consistent description of their discrepancy. Which quantities are shared must be determined independently. Choosing them solely to secure agreement would deprive the proposal of empirical force.

The minimal-self proposal concerns the perceiver's position and condition within the situation, such as the cup's location relative to a body that can reach it. The encoding could be implicit and need carry no autobiographical narrative. Its proposed necessity is philosophical: even an unreflective episode has a perspective. Gluing by itself requires no self-encoding, and reconstructability alone supplies no functional role. Altering the encoded hand or viewpoint while holding the external scene fixed should change estimates of the perceiver's relation to the cup as the readout predicts. Whether that represented perspective is experienced remains a further question.

\subsection{What a test of the full proposal must establish}
\label{sec:full-test}

Both uniqueness results concern specified objects. They choose neither the cover nor the encoding, and do not select between candidate subjects. Integrated information theory treats exclusion explicitly \cite{tononi2016}; here selection requires independent evidence about neural organization. A constant readout can contract and fix a predetermined state while discarding its input. The family criterion in Section~\ref{sec:reconstruction-family} tests the sensitivity that this construction lacks, provided the relevant states and fidelity are declared independently.

A test must separately measure coupling relative to diffusion, content compatibility and self-reconstruction, alongside an independent endpoint for experience. Meeting all three without a unified episode would challenge sufficiency. A unified episode with incompatible descriptions would challenge the unity identification; one lacking the specified self-encoding would challenge the reflexive requirement. Awakening, dreaming and dissociative states offer possible settings, but absence of report cannot by itself define absence of experience.

The next physical requirement is a calibrated geometry--coupling relation and resolved phase dynamics. The installation bound contributes a necessary resource condition where its hardware representation applies; cortical mode prices and field-mediated coupling require measurement. For the representational connection, an informative experiment asks whether a controlled change in coordination alters independently decoded content agreement, with controls for sensory information and decoder quality. The constructed-data estimator establishes feasibility for specified contents. Neural local descriptions, shared overlap encoders and the self-encoding have yet to be identified. The population-size dependence of the phase-to-content bound limits its quantitative use in the form Section~\ref{sec:content-connection} reads off the sheet, and each way around it asks the measurement for something further: a located exceptional set, before a count of disagreeing pairs bears on a named overlap; a cover whose nerve is connected and whose patch diameters are measured; or a frequency-locked population over a coupling graph whose connectivity is measured structurally rather than estimated from the activity the bound is about.

The separation results explain why these measurements must be independent. A stationary distribution leaves several recovery mechanisms indistinguishable. Equal phase order can accompany incompatible descriptions or different task information. The tested plasticity rule preserves coherence while losing its designated template. Each finding concerns the specified model and observable; their role is to delimit the evidence needed for the proposed connections.

\subsection{Explanatory scope}
\label{sec:scope}

Integrated-information, global-workspace and predictive-processing accounts organize unity, access and modelling differently \cite{tononi2016,baars1997,dehaene2014,friston2010}. The present proposal contributes a candidate physical interaction and conditional relations between local content and self-encoding. Gluing and reconstruction permit multiple substrates; the electromagnetic commitment enters through physical calibration. That permission is wider than the unconditional results of Section~\ref{sec:unconditional} alone. The conditional chain is substrate-neutral in the same way: its field condition asks for a jointly continuous kernel on a normalized substrate, a coupling equal to that kernel's double average, a noise that is the field's own, and no single site carrying more than half the coupling---and none of these distinguishes an electromagnetic kernel from any other continuous mean-field kernel. The site condition is the only one with physical flavour, and it holds vacuously wherever the substrate has no atoms (\texttt{no\_\allowbreak site\_\allowbreak dominates}), which is the continuum case the electromagnetic hypothesis is about. The chain therefore transfers to extracellular ion diffusion, to gap junctions modelled as a density, or to a mechanical or optical medium, modulo the calibration of Section~\ref{sec:scaling}. What carries the electromagnetic identification is that calibration together with the extracellular-geometry argument of Section~\ref{sec:prediction}, which is the one place a field equation enters. The formalization checks implications under their hypotheses, and its witnesses establish realizability on declared models. Two things stand outside that result and are not supplied by it: the eight hypotheses are discharged for toy components rather than for cortex, and the identification of the glued state with unity and of its reconstruction with a minimal self is a further commitment that the eight do not contain.

The explanatory question is whether the specified relations occur in neural activity and matter for experience. In the cup example, this means establishing how coordination helps maintain one represented situation and how the perceiver's place within it contributes to the episode. The proposal identifies candidate correlates and tests of that identification. Why those relations would be accompanied by experience remains unexplained.
