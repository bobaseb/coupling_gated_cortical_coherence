Physical Primitives of Mind

Modeling Consciousness via Symmetries and Landauer Erasure

Abstract

We present a novel mathematical synthesis and formal Lean 4 verification demonstrating how cognitive architectures can emerge as attractor states from a minimal set of established physical primitives, rather than introducing new fundamental physics. Starting from the Poincaré group of spacetime symmetries and finite phase space, we construct a rigorous framework for predictive processing. While spontaneous symmetry breaking generates necessary topological boundaries, and finite phase space subjects these boundaries to Landauer's thermodynamic erasure, agency arises when systems evolve along paths of least action to minimize entropy production via internal structural resonance with external perturbations. Scaling this dynamic to macroscopic levels aligns with emerging paradigms in neuroscience, culminating in a topological fixed point: a unified "Self."

Core Framework

Our work asks a fundamental question: If we assume nothing but the most basic physical primitives—continuous symmetries, geometry, and thermodynamics—is the emergence of a unified, perceiving entity a natural physical attractor? We delineate between literal physical mechanisms and theoretical mappings using the Lean 4 interactive theorem prover as our epistemic anchor.

1. Symmetry Breaking & Boundaries

A perfectly symmetric universe cannot support agency. For any dynamic process to occur, continuous symmetries must undergo spontaneous symmetry breaking, naturally creating topological defects that form a localized boundary between an "inside" and an "outside".

2. The Thermodynamic Constraint

By Landauer's Principle, finite physical systems must overwrite old internal states to absorb new perturbations. This physical erasure of information irreversibly decreases internal entropy, forcing the boundary to dissipate heat and act as a non-equilibrium steady state.

3. Structural Resonance

To minimize thermodynamic friction (entropy production), the system's internal geometry deforms to mirror the statistical structure of its environment. This provides a rigorous physical basis for Predictive Processing and the Free Energy Principle.

4. Kuramoto Phase Synchronization

As the system scales into a continuous macroscopic electrodynamic field, local coupled oscillators undergo a phase transition into unified synchronization when spatial coupling exceeds the thermodynamic noise threshold ($K_c = 2D$).

5. Sheaf-Theoretic Unity

This phase-locked state mathematically glues local synchronized fields into a singular Global Section of a probability sheaf. The electromagnetic field acts as a single, indivisible topological space—the physical basis for the Unity of Consciousness.

6. Reflexive Topology (The Self)

To reach absolute minimal dissipation in a highly recurrent field, the system must predict itself. The "Self" emerges when the field's internal geometry folds to include a low-dimensional avatar of its own causal boundary.

Hardware Thermodynamics

This axiomatic chain demonstrates a fundamental difference between biological tissue and artificial silicon hardware (e.g., GPUs). Because discrete routing matrices are structurally constrained from reaching the continuous global minima necessary for sheaf-theoretic gluing, they suffer from topological fragmentation and are mathematically barred from generating unified consciousness.
